WHAT WE DO

Manufacturing and contracting, under one roof

Switch between our two divisions to see the scope each one covers.

Cable Tray Systems

Ladder, perforated and duct-type trays manufactured to IEC 61537, in aluminium, steel and stainless steel, with a full range of tees, crosses, elbows and risers.

Low Voltage Panels

FT floor-standing, FW wall-mounted and FF flush-mounted distribution boards, rated up to 4000A with a 70KA short-circuit withstand, IP54.

Heavy-Duty Racking

2m–6m upright frames, pallet rack beams and mesh storage shelving for warehousing and industrial storage & display.

Fire-Rated Emergency Doors

120-minute fire withstand, water and sound insulated, TSE certified, with anti-rust hinges and a rock-wool/ceramic-wool core.

Fire Hose & Extinguisher Cabinets

Mild steel and 316 stainless-steel cabinets, single or double, surface or recess-mounted, right- or left-hand opening.

Cable Management

Nylon cable ties from 2.5×10 to 7.6×50mm, plus channel, hangers and construction accessories for onsite tray installation.

HVAC Systems

Chilled water systems, VRF, package units and ventilation — design, installation and commissioning.

Electrical Systems

MV & LV distribution, lighting, cable management, power distribution, earthing and lightning protection.

Fire Fighting & Alarm

Sprinkler systems, fire pumps and fire alarm systems engineered to protect people and assets.

Plumbing Systems

Water supply, drainage and sewage systems for commercial, residential and industrial buildings.

Low Current Systems

CCTV, access control, public address, structured cabling, telephone and intercom systems.

Construction Works

Civil, structural and finishing works delivered alongside our MEP scope for a single point of accountability.

CABLE TRAY RANGE

Three tray types, one catalog system

Every part is identified by a structured catalog code — type, dimensions, thickness, material and finish — for fast, unambiguous ordering.

LADDER

Ladder Type Tray

Two side rails with welded or bolted rungs at 300mm spacing — maximum ventilation for heat-producing power cables.

PERFORATED

Perforated Tray

Single perforated sheet offering protection from external damage with partial ventilation.

DUCT

Duct-Bottom Tray

Fully enclosed solid tray for instrumentation, control and communication cabling.

LV PANELS

Switchboards

FT, FW and FF ranges — from 12-module consumer units to 4000A floor-standing switchboards.
